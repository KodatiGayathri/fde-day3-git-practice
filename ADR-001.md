# ADR-001: Link Shipment to Supplier through Purchase Order

## Status
Accepted

## Context
A supplier gets many orders, and one order can come in many shipments. We needed to know which order each shipment belongs to.

## Decision
Supplier -> Purchase Order -> Shipment. Each shipment is linked to a PO, and the PO is linked to the supplier.

## Alternatives Considered
Linking Shipment directly to Supplier. Rejected because we would not know which order a shipment belongs to.

## Consequences
Finding a shipment's supplier needs one extra step (through the PO), but we get clear tracking of every order.
